## About me
Mmathapelo Matsobe is an Administrative/Student Assistant at the North-West University Potchefstroom Campus, known for her dedication, resilience and ambition to grow in the data science industry. With a strong statistics and mathematics foundation and a passion for continuous learning, she is actively working toward transitioning into a remote data science role by January 2027.

Mmathapelo is also a proud mother to a beautiful baby girl, a creative spirit and a vibrant writer and poetess. She just completed her first poetry book that will soon be published on Amazon Kindle. 

Peculiar and introverted, she describes herself as a "weirdo who loves reading," someone who talks to nature more than to people, does not quite grasp sarcasm, and is "smart but emotionally dumb." She also has "middle child syndrome", meaning not a lot of people understands her personality.

Her journey reflects both ambition and a steadfast heart, bringing a unique combination of curiosity, wit, grit and authenticity to every challenge she faces.
